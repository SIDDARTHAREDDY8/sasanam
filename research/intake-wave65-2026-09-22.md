## Applied learnings

**(A) "Record at the boundary; the action and its record should be the
same transaction"** (Dev Verma, Medium, Jul 2026 — "AI Agent Audit
Trails: Prove What Your AI Did"). Two of its five properties landed
directly in the wave-65 design: (1) record at the boundary — our ack
record is appended inside `acknowledge()`, the one funnel every unwrap
passes through, not in each consumer's code; (2) non-optional
transaction — the record-then-unwrap ordering makes the record and the
unwrap one atomic step (a raising sink withholds the payload; there is
no unhappy path that unwraps unrecorded through the public API).
External validation that the ordering choice is the industry's stated
best practice, not our invention.

**(B) "The chain seals whatever the audit writer is handed"**
(levaj2000/ai-identity trust-model.md §"Tamper-evident, not
tamper-proof": "If an event is falsified *upstream* of the writer, the
chain faithfully and verifiably records the falsehood. Chain integrity
proves a record has not changed *since it was written*; authenticity at
capture time is the job of the signing layers above."). This is the
exact honest-limits framing for our ack-record honesty section: the
hash chain proves the ack record hasn't changed since the drain; the
`consumer` field's authenticity at capture is self-attested (the
documented gap). Their "What a valid attestation does NOT prove" table
is the model for our "what acknowledged does NOT claim" list —
including their row "Timing within the session: producer-reported",
which matches our decision to stamp ack-time `ts` at the sink (ack
time) separately from the log's drain-time `ts`.

**(C) TRACE (Linux Foundation, announced ~2026-09-21)** — hardware-attested
AI runtime evidence (EAT/RATS/SCITT). Out of our $0/local scope and
threat model (no hardware root of trust in-repo), but it confirms the
direction: the industry is moving toward "evidence you can verify
without trusting the operator". Our ack trail is the software-layer
half of that story (who unwrapped unvetted data, when, in which
window) — TRACE-shaped evidence would be the deployment's hardware
half. Noted as context, not a build item.
