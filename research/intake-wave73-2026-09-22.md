## What the intake establishes (load-bearing for the wave-73 schema)

### EU AI Act Art 12 — record-keeping (Regulation 2024/1689)
- **Art 12(1):** high-risk AI systems shall *technically allow for the
  automatic recording of events ("logs") over the lifetime of the system*.
  The eu-ai-act-compliance skill (n-sig, GitHub) stresses the operative
  word: **"technical" is an architectural requirement, not a policy one** —
  logging cannot be opt-in, configurable by the agent, or dependent on the
  agent invoking a logging function. It must happen automatically.
- **Art 12(2):** logging must enable traceability appropriate to the
  intended purpose, of events relevant for (a) identifying risk situations
  / substantial modifications (Art 79(1)), (b) facilitating post-market
  monitoring (Art 72), (c) monitoring of operation (Art 26(5)). The
  operative standard is **purpose-driven event logging, not payload
  capture** — independent sources (n-sig compliance skill, capsule docs)
  agree the Act does **not** demand full prompts/outputs; recommending
  that indiscriminately creates GDPR exposure (Art 5(1)(c) data
  minimisation, Art 9 special categories).
- **Art 12(3) scope warning (load-bearing for our schema):** the minimum
  content list — (a) period of each use (start/end), (b) reference
  database checked, (c) input data leading to a match, (d) identity of
  natural persons verifying results — applies **only to Annex III
  point 1(a)** (remote biometric identification). **Do not apply the
  Art 12(3) list to systems outside Annex III 1(a)** — a common
  misreading. Our session-timing fields (period-of-use start/end) are
  justified as *general traceability* (Art 12(2)), not as an Art 12(3)
  claim; the design doc must not claim Art 12(3) compliance for agents.
- **Art 12(4) / Art 26(5):** minimum **6-month retention** (Art 26(5) puts
  the duty on deployers; healthcare/finance imply longer). This anchors
  the wave-73 retention manifest's default: 6 months minimum, per-tier.
- **Adjacent pressure:** Art 15 (robustness/cybersecurity) pushes
  tamper-evidence; Art 73 requires forensic preservability — the
  hash-chain + external anchor substrate (waves ≤72) is the honest
  technical answer to Art 15, not Art 12 itself.
- Agentlair's Art-12-for-agents mapping (piiiico/agentlair, GitHub blog)
  gives the auditor-facing vocabulary we adopt verbatim in the export:
  **who acted / when they acted / what resource they touched / what the
  outcome was**; post-market monitoring as a *live operational capability*
  (query + export the trail, hand it to a regulator — not someday, live);
  12(4) minimum retention six months.
