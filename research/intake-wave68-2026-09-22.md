## 1. Vaara — EU AI Act August 2026 doc (directly on-theme)

- **Omnibus VII is law:** Council final approval 29 June 2026;
  published in the Official Journal 24 July 2026 as Regulation (EU)
  2026/1744, in force 27 July 2026. High-risk obligations (Arts 8–27,
  incl. Art 12 record-keeping, Art 14 oversight) moved to **2 Dec
  2027** (stand-alone) / **2 Aug 2028** (embedded in products).
  **Article 50 transparency + GPAI enforcement live from 2 Aug
  2026** — no grace period for 50(1).
- **Commission guidance C(2026) 5054 final (20 July 2026), para
  31:** an AI agent must disclose its artificial nature **and the
  person on whose behalf it acts**, covering delegation of authority
  and accountability, at key steps (authorisation, reporting,
  validation), every new interaction, and when relying on other AI
  systems' output. This is an *attribution* obligation in
  regulation's clothing — "on whose behalf" is exactly the field
  wave-67/68 put in the chain (consumer/token_id) and the export.
- **Export design (vaara `export-article50`):** disclosure recorded
  as one more event in the *signed, hash-chained trail*; one command
  produces a zip = signed trail + `article50_report.md` (counts, the
  events, per-session timing coverage) + `README_FOR_AUTHORITY.md` —
  "a cover note written for the market-surveillance officer
  receiving it — what is inside, how to verify it offline without
  trusting the operator, and **what it does and does not
  establish**." Verify offline with pubkey; no trust in the operator
  required.
- **Honesty language matches our F29 posture:** vaara's report
  "states plainly what it proves (the record was made then and has
  not been altered) and what it does not (that pixels rendered, or
  that wording met accessibility requirements)." Our
  validity-at-time note (wave-68 lane 1) is the same pattern applied
  to token validity: proves validity-at-time, does not prove current
  validity.
- **Authority reference field:** vaara's agent disclosure carries
  `authority_ref="mandate-2026-041"` and an optional eIDAS-style
  attestation pinned by SHA-256 — the direct analogue of our
  `token_id` as the authority's handle on the claim. Their design
  keeps the attestation *optional and pinned by hash*, same shape as
  ours (optional token, public id in the chain).
- **"Two years of runway … is time to build the record-keeping habit
  cheaply, not a reason to ignore it… buying evidence retroactively
  is precisely the thing evidence cannot be."** — supports the
  founder's evidence bet sequencing (evidence first, commercial
  pipeline later).

## 2. Sanction (ericlovold/sanction) — EU AI Act crosswalk

- `GET /api/v1/audit/export` — a **signed, hash-chained** snapshot;
  "altering, dropping, or reordering any entry breaks the chain."
  Approvals record **who** decided, **when**, and **why**
  (`resolvedBy`, timestamp, rationale) — Art 14 evidence.
- Maps to our export: their "who decided" is our attribution field;
  their export is the artifact ours parallels. No new surface; one
  more independent confirmation that signed hash-chained export +
  named deciders is the converging industry shape.

## 3. acgs-lite — EU AI Act runtime enforcement demos

- Article12Logger (tamper-evident record chain) + AttestationRegistry
  (signed receipt for the same decision); the receipt is tampered
  with post-issuance to show verification fails.
- **Takeaway (theirs):** "automatic, tamper-evident logging and
  independently verifiable attestation receipts are two different
  integrity mechanisms — a chain that detects internal tampering,
  and a signature that detects external tampering of a single
  exported artifact. **Both must hold** for a credible audit trail."
- Maps to our posture: our chain is the internal-tamper mechanism;
  the export's offline verifiability (vaara-style, pubkey) is the
  external one. Our F29 dev-key banner is the honest statement of
  where the external mechanism currently stands (dev key = anyone
  can forge; prod KMS is the standing priority).

## 5. Operational-evidence checklist (Ellie Do, Medium, Aug 2026)

- Strong evidence record should show: model/agent version, input,
  data access, **user identity**, **permissions**, output, **human
  review (who approved/rejected, when)**, action taken, timestamp,
  exception handling. EU AI Act Art 26: deployers keep
  automatically generated logs under their control, minimum **six
  months**.
- Maps 1:1 to our export fields (attribution/token_id/ts +
