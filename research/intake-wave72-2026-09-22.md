## Learnings applied

**L1. The hashedrekord 0.0.1 envelope shape, corroborated.**
Request: `{kind: "hashedrekord", apiVersion: "0.0.1", spec: {data: {hash:
{algorithm, value}}, signature: {content, publicKey: {content}}}}` —
matches `RekorBackend.submit`'s outbound body exactly. Response:
uuid-keyed entry with `body` (base64 canonical entry), `integratedTime`,
`logIndex`, `logID`, `verification.inclusionProof.{hashes,rootHash,
treeSize,logIndex}` and `verification.signedEntryTimestamp` — matches
`_receipt_from_entry`'s parse. The digest-only signature
(`signature.content` = base64 Ed25519 sig over the 32-byte digest, not
over an artifact) is the documented purpose of the hashedrekord type —
it is the privacy pin's protocol justification: Rekor never needs the
statement.

**L2. The pin is live-verified; the wire path is not.** The GET half of
the Rekor binding (reachability + shard identity) is verified against
production today. The POST half (submit + inclusion-proof shape for a
real tree) rests on docs-reading plus the recording-transport unit test
— which is why `RekorBackend.verify_receipt`'s RFC 6962 path got a
same-wave implementation fix at closeout (the closeout battery caught a
proof-consumption-order bug that no live exercise had ever touched).

**L3. No new paid/tooling dependencies.** stdlib `urllib` only, per the
design; nothing in the intake suggests otherwise.
